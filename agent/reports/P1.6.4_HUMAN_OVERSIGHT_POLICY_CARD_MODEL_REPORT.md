## Summary

Implemented the second specific typed policy card model: `HumanOversightPolicyCard`. It defines human/operator oversight semantics for AurelCore policy cards — what human involvement is required before, during, or after an action (approval, explicit confirmation, denial, etc.). It bridges risk tier → oversight level → approval/confirmation requirement → escalation. It is declarative, closed-world, deterministic, and hash-ready.

## Known Limitations

- Approval/confirmation semantics are defined but not executable
- Escalation triggers are defined but not evaluated (trigger evaluation belongs to later policy resolver)
- No runtime integration with AurelFlow or AurelExec
- No governance board or delegation resolution
- Schema version "1.0" only; no migration path yet
- evidence_requirements are tuples but not enforced at runtime
