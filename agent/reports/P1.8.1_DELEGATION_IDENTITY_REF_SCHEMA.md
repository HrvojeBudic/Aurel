## 1. Purpose

P1.8.1 creates the stable canonical identity/reference layer for delegations so later P1.8 modules can refer to a delegation without executing it, approving it, enforcing it, verifying it, or treating it as authority.

**Core law:** DelegationRef identifies delegation. It does not approve, execute, verify, enforce, prove non-repudiation, or activate agent identity mesh.

## 13. Boundary Reminder

DelegationRef identifies delegation. It does not approve, execute, verify, enforce, or prove non-repudiation.

DelegationIdentity binds identity metadata. It does not activate delegation, verify delegation, or prove non-repudiation.

DelegationRefBinding describes reference linkage. It is not verification, trace proof, or approval.

Reference is not authority. Identity is not execution. Binding is not approval.
