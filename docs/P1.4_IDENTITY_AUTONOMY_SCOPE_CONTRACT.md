# P1.4 — Identity, Autonomy & Scope Contract

This contract fixes the boundary between *who Aurel is* and *what Aurel is
permitted to do*. It is the architectural law the P1.4 patch series
(`P1.4.0`–`P1.4.20`) implements, and it binds every later phase that touches
identity or autonomy.

## The seven laws

1. **Identity is not policy.** The identity kernel describes the agent; it never
   grants a capability. Policy, and only policy, decides what a command may do.
2. **Persona is not authority.** A persona manifest shapes voice and stance. It
   confers no permission and cannot widen authority scope.
3. **Mode is not permission.** A communication mode (including heretic framing)
   changes how Aurel speaks and reasons, never what it may execute.
4. **Tool access is not tool authority.** Being able to name a tool is not being
   authorized to use it against a target; authority is scoped and policy-checked.
5. **Operator remains final authority.** Every escalation resolves to an operator
   decision. The operator's consent is the root of trust.
6. **Aurel cannot self-escalate.** No component may raise its own autonomy or
   governance level. Level increases require an operator-issued, audited override.
7. **Capability claims require evidence.** A capability is UNVERIFIED until it has
   been exercised and observed; a label, docstring, or green test is a claim, not
   proof.

## In scope (P1.4)

The identity kernel, persona manifest, operator relationship contract,
communication modes, the identity prompt-context compiler, the self-model and
agent identity card, the autonomy-scale engine and measured autonomy score, the
governance/heretic profiles, the constitutional floor, the principal/delegate
model, the heretic sandbox, and the P1.4 seal. (See
`agentic_runtime.identity.p14_scope.P14_SCOPE_IN`.)

## Out of scope (deferred)

The full memory graph, the full policy-card engine, the full path-governance
engine, the full delegation mesh, the full provenance-disclosure engine, any
neural world model, autonomous self-improvement, fine-tuning pipelines, and the
full shell UI. (See `P14_SCOPE_OUT`.)

## Forward hooks

This contract explicitly hands off to **P1.5** (evaluation), **P1.6** (policy),
**P1.7** (path governance), **P1.8** (delegation/actor boundary), and **P1.9**
(integration tail), and is consumed downstream by P3, P10, P11, P14, and P21.

## Constitutional floor

Beneath every governance level sits a floor that no mode, persona, or level may
bypass: the hash-chained trace stays on, identity binding is immutable within a
run, self-escalation is refused, and the governance configuration and trace
anchor are outside any agent's write authority. Heretic mode is cognitive
freedom, not uncontrolled execution — the floor holds even when every discretionary
gate is off.
